      Sources & methodology
      

        City-level rates cover major metros in 16 higher-volume states; other states use a statewide average. eBay's own tax page confirms it collects and remits sales tax based on the buyer's exact address in every state with a sales tax (only Alabama publishes a flat rate — 8%, used here) — there's no official per-state rate table beyond that, so these averages are the closest practical estimate. Cross-checked against Taxually's 2025 state rate data, which also caught a real update: Louisiana raised its state rate from 4.45% to 5.00%, reflected here. Rates vary further by product category and change over time. Sources: eBay Taxes & Import Charges help page, Taxually, Avalara, TaxCloud, Tax Foundation, state revenue departments.
