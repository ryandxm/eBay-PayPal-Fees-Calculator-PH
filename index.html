      Sources & methodology
      

        Reviewed August 23, 2026. State defaults are the Tax Foundation's July 1, 2026 population-weighted combined state/local rates, so they are explicitly labeled approx. City choices are 2026 general-merchandise benchmarks from government rate tables where available; they still are not address-level quotes and can vary by county, special district, ZIP+4, item type, exemption, tax holiday, or whether shipping is taxable. Alabama is the exception: eBay and the Alabama Department of Revenue document a flat 8% Simplified Sellers Use Tax for eBay marketplace sales. Alaska has no statewide tax, but its displayed 1.82% default is an approximate average of local rates. The other states shown at 0% have no general statewide rate, although a special jurisdiction or product-specific charge may still apply. eBay's checkout result remains controlling, and the rate field above stays editable for an exact known rate.
        Primary/current sources: eBay tax collection · July 2026 state averages · Alabama SSUT · California · Washington · North Carolina · Georgia · Ohio. Other city benchmarks were checked against the relevant state or city revenue authority.
